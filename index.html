Privacy policy for Moonlit Math

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Moonlit Math is a maths game for children. This policy explains what the app does with information.

Who we are

Moonlit Math is published by its developer for the App Store. The app has no account system and no sign-in.

What the app stores

Everything the game remembers stays on the device. That includes:

  • which maths facts have been practised
  • sound and music on or off
  • the chosen buddy
  • trophies, level, and gear from the trophy shop
  • parent choices: a daily play-time limit, which topics are allowed, the hardest difficulty, and whether Surprise Mix is on
  • how many minutes have been counted today, so the play limit can last across separate opens and start again the next day

The app does not ask for a child’s name, email, photo, location, contacts, or a password. The trophy shop spends trophies earned by playing. It does not charge money and does not offer real-world purchases.

What the app does not do

  • No account and no sign-in
  • No advertising
  • No third-party analytics or tracking
  • No selling or sharing of information
  • No microphone, camera, location, contacts, or photo access
  • No links, share sheets, email, or review prompts that leave the app

Sound effects and music play on the device. The app does not record the microphone.

Network

Moonlit Math is meant to work from the copy installed on the device. It does not upload practice or settings.

The app includes Expo’s update support. After a failed launch, the app may contact Expo (https://u.expo.dev) to see whether a replacement copy of the app has been published. That check identifies the app and its version. It does not include the child’s practice, trophies, or parent settings. A normal launch uses the copy already installed and does not wait on the network.

Children

The app is made for children. Parent settings, including the play-time limit and the reset control, sit behind a grown-up check that is harder than the maths the game teaches. Resetting progress clears practice, trophies, and today’s play time on this device. It does not delete the parent settings.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app can be sent to saucers_pregame_01@icloud.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it. The app does not notify an account, because there is no account.
