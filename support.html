Frequently asked questions

How do I open the Parents area?

Tap the small lock on the title screen. A grown-up check asks you to type the answer to a multiplication that's harder than the maths in the game. Three wrong answers lock the check for 45 seconds.

How do I set a daily play limit?

In the Parents area, choose a daily play time of 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes, or turn it off. When the time is used up, the game shows a "Time to rest, buddy" screen. The limit starts fresh the next day.

Can I choose which topics and difficulty my child plays?

Yes. In the Parents area you can turn topics on or off, including Surprise Mix, and set the hardest level your child can pick.

How do I turn sound or music off?

You can switch sound effects and music off in the Parents area.

How do I reset progress?

Choose Reset progress in the Parents area and confirm. This clears practice, trophies and today's play time on this device. Parent settings and the chosen buddy are kept.

Does Moonlit Math have ads, accounts or in-app purchases?

No. There are no ads, no accounts or sign-in, and no in-app purchases. The trophy shop only uses trophies earned by playing.

Is my child's data collected?

No. Progress and settings stay on your device. Read our privacy policy for details.

Will progress move to a new device?

Progress is saved only on the device where the game is played, so it doesn't carry over to another device.
